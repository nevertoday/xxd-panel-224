## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design reconstructs the memorable subject from irregular Chinese porcelain shards with real glaze, biscuit breaks, cracks, missing corners and gaps; fragmentation and reassembly are part of the narrative. It is not a photographic porcelain filter, a filled antique display, a complete scene, cheap national-tide pattern stacking, 3D plastic, or a templated collage. Keep small-stamp composition and abundant porcelain-white negative space. Extract glaze colours from the source rather than defaulting to a fixed blue-and-white palette.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-224/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
